Put these two files in this folder (both from the SAME download):

    sqlite3.c
    sqlite3.h

How to get them (free, no account needed):
 1. Open https://www.sqlite.org/download.html
 2. Under "Source Code", download  sqlite-amalgamation-XXXXXXX.zip
 3. Extract it and copy sqlite3.c and sqlite3.h into this folder.

The first run of build.bat compiles sqlite3.c once into sqlite3.o (about a minute).
